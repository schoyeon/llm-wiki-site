# Surf2Spot: A Surface-Informed Geometry-Aware Model for Predicting Binder and Nanobody Design Hotspots
**Authors:** Anwen Zhao, Yu Li, Tianhao Wu, Ran Fu, ..., Xiangfeng Wang
**Source:** bioRxiv, 2026
**DOI:** 10.64898/2026.01.23.700806
## Summary
Surf2Spot은 sequence embedding, structural features, protein surface properties를 통합하여 PPI 및 nanobody-antigen interaction hotspot을 예측하는 deep learning 모델. PPI/NAI 데이터셋에서 F1-score 45.9-46.9%, AUPRC 43.2% 향상. RFdiffusion/BindCraft 파이프라인에 통합시 4배 높은 design throughput과 향상된 binding affinity.
## Key Points
- Sequence + structure + surface property 통합 모델
- PPI 및 **nanobody-antigen** hotspot 모두 예측
- RFdiffusion/BindCraft에 통합 가능 → 4x throughput 향상
- Case study: NbPDS1, VdPDA1에서 실험적 hotspot과 일치
