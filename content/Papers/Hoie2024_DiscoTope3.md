# DiscoTope-3.0: improved B-cell epitope prediction using inverse folding latent representations
**Authors:** Magnus H. Høie, Frederik S. Gade, Julie M. Johansen, Charlotte Würtzen, Ole Winther, Morten Nielsen & Paolo Marcatili
**Journal:** Frontiers in Immunology, 2024
**DOI:** 10.3389/fimmu.2024.1322712
## Summary
DiscoTope-3.0은 ESM-IF1의 inverse folding latent representation을 활용한 B-cell epitope 예측 도구. Solved, relaxed, predicted structure 모두에서 높은 성능. RCSB와 AlphaFoldDB 연동으로 2억+ 단백질에 대한 large-scale 예측 가능.
## Key Points
- ESM-IF1 inverse folding representation + positive-unlabelled learning
- Predicted structure (AF2)에서도 높은 성능 유지
