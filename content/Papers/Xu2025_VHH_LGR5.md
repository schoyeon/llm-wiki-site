# De Novo Computational Design of VHH Nanobodies Against LGR5
**Authors:** Chenrui Xu, Yifan Li, Trang Nguyen, Yi Zhou, ..., Per Greisen
**Source:** bioRxiv, 2025 (BioMap Research)
**DOI:** 10.64898/2025.12.02.691880
## Summary
LGR5 (GPCR) 를 target으로 epitope-directed VHH nanobody를 de novo 설계하는 fully computational pipeline. Generative backbone modeling + DL sequence optimization + iterative experimental feedback으로 3 design-test-learn cycle을 거쳐 nanomolar affinity (Kd), 높은 thermal stability (Tm > 65°C) 달성. Cryo-EM으로 atomic-level 검증 (RMSD ≈ 2.2Å).
## Key Points
- GPCR (LGR5) 대상 — 기존 방법으로 어려운 타겟
- 3 design-test-learn cycles, millions → epitope-specific binders
- Cryo-EM 검증: RMSD ≈ 2.2Å
- BioMap Research (Beijing/Palo Alto)
