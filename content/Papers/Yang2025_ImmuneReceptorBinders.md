# Design of high-affinity binders to immune modulating receptors for cancer immunotherapy
**Authors:** Wei Yang, Derrick R. Hicks, Agnidipta Ghosh, ..., Andrew P. Hinck & David Baker
**Journal:** Nature Communications, 2025
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-025-57192-z
## Summary
Cancer immunotherapy 타겟 (TGFβRII, CTLA-4, PD-L1) 에 대한 helical concave scaffold 기반 high-affinity binder 설계. Convex Ig fold surface에 결합하도록 설계된 helical scaffold → picomolar affinity 달성. Co-crystal structure로 design model 검증.
## Key Points
- Helical concave scaffold → convex immune receptor surface에 결합
- TGFβRII, CTLA-4, PD-L1 타겟
- Low nanomolar ~ picomolar affinity (experimental optimization 후)
- Co-crystal structure 검증
