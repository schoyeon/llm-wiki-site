# De novo design of high-affinity binders of bioactive helical peptides
**Authors:** Susana Vázquez Torres, Philip J. Y. Leung, Preetham Venkatesh, ..., David Baker
**Journal:** Nature, Vol 626, 2024
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-06953-1
## Summary
RFdiffusion을 확장하여 helical peptide에 결합하는 단백질을 de novo 설계. PTH, glucagon, GCG 등 bioactive peptide hormone에 대한 picomolar-affinity binder 설계 성공. Partial diffusion으로 flexible peptide target에 대한 설계 가능. Mass spectrometry로 PTH/glucagon 검출 실현.
## Key Points
- RFdiffusion + partial diffusion → flexible peptide target 대응
- Picomolar affinity binder for helical peptides
- PTH, glucagon 검출용 biosensor 응용
- Co-crystal structure 검증
