# FTMAP: extended protein mapping with user-selected probe molecules
**Authors:** Chi Ho Ngan, Tanggis Bohnuud, Scott E. Mottarella, Dmitri Beglov, Elizabeth A. Villar, David R. Hall, Dima Kozakov & Sandor Vajda
**Journal:** Nucleic Acids Research, 2012
**DOI:** 10.1093/nar/gks441
## Summary
FTMAP은 small organic molecule probe를 사용하여 단백질 표면의 binding hot spot을 computational하게 매핑하는 웹 서버. 16개 probe molecule을 target에 docking하여 clustering → hot spot 예측. NMR/X-ray 실험 결과와 좋은 일치.
## Key Points
- Fragment-based approach: 16개 small molecule probe 사용
- CHARMM potential + continuum electrostatics
- Binding hot spot = 여러 probe cluster가 겹치는 영역
- 웹 서버: ftmap.bu.edu/param
